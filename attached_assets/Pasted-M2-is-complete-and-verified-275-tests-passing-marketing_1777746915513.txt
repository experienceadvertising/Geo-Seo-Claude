Mobile responsiveness is a stretch goal here, not a hard gate. The admin panel is desktop-first (you'll mostly use it from a laptop). Sidebar collapses on mobile per Ticket 3.1, but I won't reject 3.x for sub-optimal phone rendering. Don't ship visually broken phone layouts though.
