# Launch roadmap

## Now: make paid traffic measurable and trustworthy

- Consent-aware GA4, Google Ads, Meta, and LinkedIn tracking
- First-touch and last-touch campaign attribution
- Signup, first audit, and checkout funnel events
- Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Google data-use disclosure
- Accurate free-month language across the acquisition flow
- Automatic continuation from landing-page URL entry into the first audit
- Campaign setup and validation guide

Success measure: cost per first audit completed, not cost per account created.

## Next: improve activation and retention

- Example audit that shows the depth and credibility of recommendations before signup
- Weekly email digest covering score changes, new issues, and the next best action
- Recommendation status tracking: planned, in progress, completed, or dismissed
- Before and after score history tied to completed recommendations
- Clear empty states that direct new users to one useful next step

Success measure: new accounts completing an audit and returning within seven days.

## Then: build the agency growth loop

- Shareable read-only reports
- Agency branding and client-ready exports
- Scheduled client reports
- Multi-client overview with risk and opportunity alerts
- Referral and partner workflow connected to attributable signups

Success measure: reports shared, additional client sites added, and paid upgrades.

## Advertising launch structure

Start with exact and phrase match Google Search campaigns around high-intent terms such as AEO audit, AI visibility checker, ChatGPT citation tracker, and answer engine optimization tool. Send each group to the closest existing landing page. Keep the first test controlled, evaluate search terms frequently, and optimize toward completed first audits.
